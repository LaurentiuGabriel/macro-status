Impact ratings. The Investing.com economic calendar rates each event's expected volatility with a one-to-three bar/"bull" scale. The eight high-impact (three-bar) releases shown here — Nonfarm Payrolls, CPI, the Fed interest-rate decision, GDP growth, the unemployment rate, Core PCE, retail sales and initial jobless claims — are the biggest market movers. The seven medium-impact (two-bar) gauges across housing (building permits, housing starts), industry (industrial production, durable-goods orders, capacity utilization) and productivity (nonfarm productivity, unit labor costs) round out the macro picture.

Data provenance. Investing.com does not publish free bulk historical downloads and blocks scraping, but its high-impact events are the official U.S. BLS, BEA, Census Bureau and Federal Reserve releases. Those exact series are republished by FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data), which is the source used here. Each figure is transformed to match how it is reported on the calendar — year-over-year for CPI and Core PCE, month-over-month change for payrolls and retail sales, and as-published for the rest. Rebuild the dataset any time with python scripts/fetch_data.py.

For information only — not investment advice. The 2020 spikes across most series are the genuine COVID-19 shock, not data errors.
